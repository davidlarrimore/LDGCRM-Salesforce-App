What would unblock the most

1. The 155 unmatched Accounts

155 Airtable Account rows don't match an Account in Salesforce. Most are duplicates within Airtable itself — Army alongside Department of the Army, and similar.

This is the item that cascades. It directly accounts for roughly 275 of the 357 held-back records: an Opportunity can't migrate without its Account, an Application can't migrate without its Partner Account, and every link and note attached to those follows.

2. Contact names

1,054 of 1,535 contacts have no name in Airtable. Salesforce requires one, so the migration derives it from the email address where it can.

3. Salesforce settings — one of three now done

The partner-portal team fields were unblocked on 14 August. They had been held back because Salesforce was configured to allow each team name to appear on only one Application, while in practice one team runs many — DOI - FWS - ECOS runs 54.

Not yet migrating

Meetings — 1,848 Airtable rows. Airtable records a date but no time. Loading them as calendar events would mean inventing a time for every meeting that ever happened. The agreed approach is to sync real calendar data into Salesforce and match Airtable's meetings onto it, which depends on a Salesforce configuration decision rather than on migration work.

Confidence

This load ran twice from an empty sandbox, the second time against a freshly pulled copy of Airtable. Both runs produced identical results — the same 8,734 records, the same 13 rejections, the same 357 held back. The process is repeatable and takes about 19 minutes as a single command.

The remaining work before production is a rehearsal in a second sandbox, the three Salesforce settings above, and a decision on Meetings.
